# Monetization

## Who pays

| Side | Model | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Companies (primary)** | Higher willingness to pay; recruiting budget already exists | Align with who captures hiring efficiency |
| **Job seekers** | **Freemium** — free core matching / auto-apply-like flow; optional paid premium later | Avoid taxing the side that already spends months applying |

---

## Preferred pricing shape

**Performance-based:** pay when value is delivered — e.g. qualified match shared, interview booked, or similar milestones (exact events TBD).

### Competitive target

Aim to be **roughly 5–10× cheaper** than current listing costs (~2–3k CHF per position on classic portals — validate).

Value story: not “another listing slot,” but **qualified introductions** that cut screen time.

### Alternatives still on the table

- Success fee / pay-per-qualified-lead  
- Subscription with caps  
- Hybrid (small access fee + performance)

Break-even assumptions and unit economics need a dedicated sketch after niche + funnel assumptions exist.

---

## Cost blocks to model

- Matching quality & ML/ops  
- Support for both sides  
- Compliance / privacy  
- Two-sided liquidity marketing  
- Feed/API fees, crawl ops, quotas  
- Integration & parser maintenance  

---

## Retention & LTV

Career-gap tools and profile upkeep between jobs:

- Lower reacquisition cost  
- Better data quality over time  
- Optional premium: deeper path analysis, partner course recommendations  

---

## Long-term data monetization

Unique structured marketplace data can become a strategic asset:

| Product direction | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Market intelligence for employers | “If you raise salary / loosen constraint X, expected candidate volume changes by Y” |
| Salary benchmarking | Anonymized bands by role / city / seniority |
| Research / institutes | Anonymized statistics |
| Adjacent partners | Certificate / training providers when gaps are common |
| Advertising | Possible later; keep secondary so it doesn’t poison trust |

**Guardrails:** anonymization, cohort minimums, no leaking individual employer bars via reverse engineering, CH/EU compliance.
