# Go-to-market & niche

## Bootstrap approach

Start with supply that already exists; earn the right to ask companies for richer first-party profiles.

```text
1. Crawl / ingest listings from main Swiss platforms
   (+ selective company career pages where needed)
        ↓
2. Match against early seeker profiles
        ↓
3. Forward relevant candidates to companies
   (prove value without requiring them to list on us first)
        ↓
4. Use that track record to onboard richer company profiles
        ↓
5. Long-term: API so companies push career-site listings directly
```

### Why this order

| Stage | Value delivered | Risk reduced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ingest + match + forward | Immediate utility for seekers; warm intro for employers | Chicken-and-egg without waiting for full employer onboarding |
| Rich employer profiles | Better matches, defensible product | Moves from “aggregator UX” to unique marketplace |
| Company API | Scalable, fresher, consented supply | Less brittle scraping; stickier B2B |

### Messaging caution

Position as “less noise, fairer process, faster interviews” — avoid unsubstantiated HR-bashing.

## Niche selection (still open)

### Criteria for a good beachhead

1. **High friction / pain** in the current system  
2. **Enough volume** of openings *and* candidates  
3. Mix of **objective + non-CV criteria** (culture, weekend work, etc.) so simple CV screeners aren’t enough  
4. **Geographic concentration** — Zurich looks attractive  
5. **High cost of a bad hire / high salaries** — makes better matching economically valuable  

### Candidates considered

| Niche | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Specialized IT / consultants** | Frequent switching for salary; strong willingness to pay for speed; culture & delivery model matter beyond CV keywords |
| **Bankers (Zurich)** | High compensation; dense geography; formal requirements + soft fit; expensive mis-hires |
| **Healthcare (nurses)** | Real pain, but strong **location constraints** may limit matching liquidity |
| **Trades** | Same location-constraint concern; may be harder for early structured matching |

**Status:** niche not locked. Decision criteria above should drive interviews and volume research before build depth.

## Seeker acquisition (first wave)

Still open in detail. Plausible levers once niche is fixed:

- Niche communities (LinkedIn/XING, Slack/Discord, alumni networks)
- Universities / training providers (especially if career-gap tips recommend certificates)
- Content: salary realism, commute-aware search, “why cover letters fail”
- Referral loops among early matched seekers

Company acquisition is expected to follow **demonstrated candidate quality** from the forward-loop, not cold “buy a listing” sales alone.

---

## Expansion path

1. Win liquidity in one niche × one geography (e.g. Zurich + chosen profession).  
2. Deepen matching quality and employer profile adoption there.  
3. Adjacent niches / cities with shared data models.  
4. API-led supply and insights products.
