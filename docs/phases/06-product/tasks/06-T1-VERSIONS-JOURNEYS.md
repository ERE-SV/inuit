## Why this exists

Before keep/cut fights, you need a shared picture: what MVP vs V2 vs V3 is *for*, how seeker and company journeys work (including bootstrap forward), and what you deliberately will not build soon.

---

## Our guideline

- **One job per version.** MVP = core match loop; V2 = retention + trust depth; V3 = scale + intelligence. If you cut it from MVP and matching breaks end-to-end, it belongs in MVP.
- **Document non-goals explicitly.** Designing a full employer ATS is out — say what would go wrong if you built it in MVP anyway.
- **Bootstrap forward is core.** Pre-qualified candidates against ingested listings before employers fully onboard — include in the company journey.
- **Indicator, not guarantee.** Match score suggests fit; UI copy and states must never promise a hire.
- **Build on the seed.** Start from [PRODUCT-CONCEPT.md](../PRODUCT-CONCEPT.md) — do not invent a second product from scratch.

| Version | Job | Rule of thumb |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MVP** | Prove the **core match loop** with beachhead liquidity | If you cut it, matching does not work end-to-end |
| **V2** | Retention + trust depth | Improves quality after the loop works |
| **V3** | Scale + intelligence | Needs MVP+V2 learning |
