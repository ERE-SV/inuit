## Learn (read before working)

### What this phase is

Turn “companies pay for performance” into **math Daniel can plan with**: who pays, what event triggers a charge, what it costs to run, and whether revenue covers that at named milestones.

The **spreadsheet holds the formulas**; [FINANCIAL-MODEL.md](FINANCIAL-MODEL.md) explains *why*. Suggested tab shape: [MODEL-OUTLINE.md](MODEL-OUTLINE.md) and [09-T3](tasks/09-T3-SPREADSHEET.md).

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning in plain language | Why it matters here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Billable event** | Exact action that triggers an invoice — not “when someone hires someday” | Gate 5 needs a named charge trigger |
| **Unit economics** | In/out **per unit** (event, seeker, or employer) | Totals alone hide whether the business works |
| **Contribution** | Revenue − variable costs for that unit | Break-even and sensitivity inputs |
| **Milestone projection** | Snapshot at a named GTM milestone | Connects finance to Gate 2 volumes |
| **Sensitivity** | Same model with a few drivers changed (best / base / worst) | Gate 5 must not be a single fantasy number |

### What to avoid

- Inventing listing prices (brief “~2–3k CHF” is a hypothesis)  
- Vague billable events  
- Revenue without GTM volumes / ops headcount timing  
- Narrative-only math (no sheet)  
- One scenario only  
- Ignoring a compliance/legal buffer (even if rough, label assumption)
