## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Calculate base-case unit economics — revenue per unit, variable costs, contribution, and break-even under a stated definition.

Example questions — skip what stays thin after an honest pass.

- What is your primary unit (per billable event / per paying employer / other)? Why?
- Base-case **revenue per unit** (CHF) and how calculated?
- **Variable costs** on that unit (extract, feed share, payment fee, …)? Sum?
- **Contribution** per unit; break-even volume or month under your stated definition?

**Start here**
