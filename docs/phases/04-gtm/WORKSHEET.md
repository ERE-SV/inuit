## Learn (read before working)

### What this phase is

**Go-to-market (GTM)** means: how you get the *first* seekers and companies in the locked beachhead to use the product, in what order, with what message, and how you know it is working. It is not a full marketing department plan and not a Figma file. GTM comes **before** deep UI so product supports the motion (especially: ingest listings → match → **forward qualified candidates** → companies claim/onboard richer profiles).

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning in plain language | Why it matters here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GTM** | Plan to reach and activate first customers on both sides of the marketplace | Empty liquidity kills product quality |
| **Seeker acquisition** | How candidates find and complete profiles in the beachhead | Supply side of matching |
| **Company forward-motion** | Prove value by sending relevant candidates *before* forcing a paid listing | Brief bootstrap order |
| **Sales shape** | Who sells, how long, self-serve vs high-touch | Sets materials and headcount later |
| **Buyer persona** | Who inside the company can say yes (HR vs hiring manager vs CEO vs board) | Wrong persona = stalled deals |
| **Milestone** | Measurable event by a date (not “get awareness”) | Gate 2 needs accountability |
| **Messaging caution** | Say “less noise, fairer process, faster interviews” — avoid unsubstantiated HR-bashing | Trust and brand later |

### Bootstrap order (from brief — do not reverse without a decision)

1. Crawl / ingest listings  
2. Match against early seeker profiles  
3. Forward relevant candidates to companies  
4. Use track record to onboard richer company profiles  
5. Later: company API push  

### Common beginner mistakes

- Writing GTM for “all of Switzerland” after a Zurich niche lock  
- Company-first “buy a listing” sales that ignore the forward-loop  
- Seeker plan with no communities / universities / niche networks named  
- Milestones without numbers or dates  
- Attacking HR in copy instead of naming the noise problem carefully  
- Starting deep product UI before Gate 2
