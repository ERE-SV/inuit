## Why this exists

Matching needs supply. A seeker plan that names no communities, universities, or niche networks is a slogan. This task makes channels, messages, and 90-day experiments concrete for the **locked** beachhead — plus what you will *not* spend time on.

---

## Our guideline

- **Beachhead-specific channels.** Names of communities, alumni groups, training providers — not “social media.”
- **Experiment + metric per channel.** One message, one 90-day test, one success metric — or don’t list the channel.
- **Minimum viable profile.** Define the thinnest seeker profile that can match — a product input for later, not a UI spec here.
- **Non-goals matter.** What you will *not* do in the first 90 days prevents “post everywhere” drift.
- **Stay inside T1 thesis.** Channels and messages must fit the locked niche × geography and T1 do/don’t lines.

Brief levers (still open until you specify): niche communities, universities/training, content (salary realism, commute, why cover letters fail), referral loops — [docs/brief/03-gtm-niche.md](../../../brief/03-gtm-niche.md).

## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Name ≥3 realistic seeker channels for the locked beachhead — each with message, experiment, metric, and explicit first-90-day non-goals.

Example questions — channels that actually exist for *this* niche.

**Channels and experiments**

- Which ≥3 seeker channels are realistic for this niche (communities, LinkedIn/XING, alumni, universities/training providers, content, referrals)? Why each fits.
- For each channel: a draft message (1–2 sentences) + a 90-day experiment + a success metric?

**Profile and loops**

- What profile-completion friction do you expect, and what is the minimum viable seeker profile for matching to start?
- Referral / loop among early matched seekers — a yes/no sketch and a metric?

**Non-goals**

- What will you *not* spend time on for seekers in the first 90 days?

You might sketch: channel · why it fits · message · experiment · metric.
