## Our guideline

- **Follow bootstrap order.** Ingest listings → match early seeker profiles → forward relevant candidates → use track record to onboard richer company profiles → later, company API push. Do not reverse without a decision.
- **Define “qualified” defensibly.** “Qualified” ≠ “we guarantee a hire.” Explain thresholds you can defend without overpromising.
- **Crawl sensitivity.** Phase 01 flagged scrape/reuse and privacy — do not market a crawl-fed product as first-party supply. Note opt-out / “stop contacting us” until counsel reviews.
- **Listing-sales is not primary.** Cold “buy a listing” should not be the main company motion yet — or argue the exception with evidence.
- **Beachhead-specific.** Named ingest sources and contact paths for the locked niche × geography.
