## Why this exists

Wrong persona stalls deals. This task names who uses the product day to day vs who can say yes in *this* beachhead (HR vs hiring manager vs CEO vs board), what they will ask, and whether sales is self-serve, founder-led high-touch, or hybrid. That sets materials and later headcount — and which company demands you accept, defer, or refuse in the first 90 days.

---

## Our guideline

- **KMU vs corporate from Phase 01.** A 20-person GmbH is not a bank TA team — pick personas that fit the locked beachhead.
- **Forward-loop first.** Sales shape should match [T3](04-T3-FORWARD-MOTION.md) — not quietly become listing-sales.
- **No invented pricing.** Do not commit to prices beyond what Phase 02 sourced.
- **Demand filter.** Accept / defer / refuse company demands (SLAs, exclusivity, volume, reporting) for the first 90 days — in writing.
- **Label inference.** Persona picks without interview evidence should be labeled **inference**.

## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Define day-to-day user vs economic buyer, persona-specific questions, sales shape, materials list, and first-90-day demand filter for the locked beachhead.

Example questions — personas that actually appear in *this* wedge.

**Personas**

- For this beachhead, who is the likely **day-to-day user** vs **economic buyer**? (HR generalist, talent acquisition, hiring manager, CEO, board — pick and justify.) **Inference** + any interview/desk evidence.
- **HR / TA questions** you must answer to win a pilot (budget owner, ATS conflict, data privacy, “another tool” fatigue)?
- **CEO / founder questions** (if SME) — speed-to-hire, cost vs agency, risk of bad hire?
- **Board / procurement questions** (if larger firm) — vendor risk, compliance, contract shape, performance vs subscription. When does this apply in your beachhead?

**Sales shape and demands**

- Sales shape: self-serve, founder-led high-touch, or hybrid? Expected cycle length (hypothesis) and materials list (one-pager, email scripts, proof of candidate quality, etc.)?
- Top **demands** companies will make (SLAs, exclusivity, volume, reporting) — which do we accept / defer / refuse in the first 90 days?

**Start here**

1. Split user vs buyer for the locked beachhead (use Phase 01 journeys).  
2. List questions by persona (HR, CEO if SME, board only if it actually applies).  
3. Pick sales shape + cycle hypothesis + materials.  
4. Sort first-90-day demands: accept / defer / refuse.  
5. Link notes from the worksheet.
