## Our guideline

- **Reuse Phase 01–02 by path.** Segment names, volume signals, and competitor context should come from prior phases — not a fresh invented study.
- **Same criteria, same scale.** All options on all six brief criteria; explain what each criterion means in one line.
- **Prove, don’t assume.** Zurich often attractive — but prove geographic concentration with evidence or labeled **inference**.
- **No national beachhead.** “Switzerland, all professions” is not an option.
- **Do not self-approve.** You propose options and scores; only Daniel locks Gate 1.

Brief criteria (use these): (1) high friction / pain, (2) enough openings *and* candidates, (3) mix of objective + non-CV criteria, (4) geographic concentration, (5) high cost of bad hire / high salaries, (6) **trust/explainability** — can this niche tolerate automated fit scores and notify-both-sides with clear transparency? ## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Design ≥3 niche × geography options and score them fairly on the six brief criteria — with evidence pointers for the leader.

Example questions — skip criteria that stay thin after an honest look.

**Options and scale**

- What ≥3 options in the form `niche × geography` (e.g. “specialized IT consultants × Zurich”)? Include brief shortlist items or justify substitutes.
- What scoring scale (e.g. 1–5), and what does each criterion mean in one line?
- How does every option score on all six brief criteria? Totals?

**Evidence and weak spots**

- For each criterion on the **leading** option: which Phase 01 or 02 fact supports the score?
- Which criterion is weakest on the leading option, and what would change your mind? (**inference** OK.)
