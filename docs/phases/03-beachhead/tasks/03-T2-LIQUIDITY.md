## Why this exists

A high-pain niche with no openings or no seekers is an empty marketplace. Gate 1’s risk discussion needs, for each option, signals on **both** sides — or an honest `unknown` plus how you would validate. Location-heavy work (nursing, trades) is the usual trap.

---

## Our guideline

- **Both sides matter.** **Liquidity** = enough *relevant* openings **and** seekers so matches happen — not openings alone.
- **Reuse prior numbers.** Pull openings from Phase 01; do not invent a second volume study. Portal hit counts are snapshots + date, not official market size.
- **Label weak proxies.** LinkedIn member counts and similar signals are **weak proxies** — say so.
- **Take location seriously.** The brief warns that nurses/trades may fail on location. Flag location-constrained options explicitly.
- **Do not self-approve.** Liquidity assumptions feed Gate 1 risks; Daniel decides whether they are good enough to lock.
