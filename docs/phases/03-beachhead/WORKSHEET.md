## Learn (read before working)

### What this phase is

A **beachhead** is the first small market you win on purpose — one **niche** (who / what profession or hiring situation) times one **geography** (where). You are not designing the whole Swiss market. You score at least three options with the brief’s criteria, check **liquidity** (enough jobs and seekers), and prepare a Gate 1 pack: a clear recommendation plus honest pros/cons so Daniel can choose. Only Daniel locks the decision in the decision log.

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning in plain language | Why it matters here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Niche** | A focused group of roles / employers / seekers with shared pain (not “all jobs in Switzerland”) | Focus creates liquidity and clearer messaging |
| **Beachhead** | Niche × geography you attack first | Program Gate 1 output |
| **Scorecard** | Same criteria, same scale, across options | Makes comparison fair for Daniel |
| **Liquidity** | Enough openings *and* candidates so matches happen | Empty marketplace = failed wedge |
| **Runner-up rejection** | Written reason you are *not* recommending an option | Shows you did not ignore alternatives |
| **Gate 1** | Decision meeting where Daniel approves / revises / rejects the beachhead | You propose; you do not self-approve |

### Beachhead criteria (from brief — use these)

1. High friction / pain in the current system  
2. Enough volume of openings *and* candidates  
3. Mix of objective + non-CV criteria (so simple CV screeners aren’t enough)  
4. Geographic concentration (Zurich often attractive — prove it)  
5. High cost of bad hire / high salaries (better matching worth paying for)  
6. **Trust / explainability** — can this niche tolerate automated fit scores and notify-both-sides with clear transparency?

Candidate niches from the brief (still open): specialized IT/consultants; bankers (Zurich); healthcare (nurses); trades — see [docs/brief/03-gtm-niche.md](../../brief/03-gtm-niche.md).

### Common beginner mistakes

- Recommending “Switzerland, all professions” as a beachhead  
- Scoring on vibes without Phase 01–02 evidence  
- Ignoring liquidity (especially location-heavy niches like nursing/trades)  
- Hiding risks of the recommended option  
- Acting as if Gate 1 is already approved
