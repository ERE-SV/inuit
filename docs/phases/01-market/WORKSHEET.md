### Key terms


| Term                | Meaning in plain language                                                           | Why it matters here                                        |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Channel**         | A place where jobs are posted or found (portal, LinkedIn, career site, RAV, agency) | Niche choice depends on where volume and pain already live |
| **KMU vs large**    | SME vs corporate hiring — often different channels, owners, and decision makers     | GTM cannot assume “HR at a bank”                           |
| **Demand / supply** | Employers needing to hire vs people looking                                         | Beachhead needs both                                       |
| **Liquidity**       | Enough relevant openings *and* seekers so matches happen                            | Empty marketplace = failed wedge                           |
| **So what**         | What a fact might mean for *this* product                                           | Turns observation into something we can build on           |
| **Inference**       | Your conclusion from facts, not a fact itself                                       | Label it so Daniel knows what's evidence vs intuition      |
