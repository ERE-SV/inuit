## Official / statistical (Tier A)

| Topic | Where to look | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CH labour market stats | [BFS — Swiss Federal Statistical Office](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/) | Employment, unemployment, sectors, **job vacancies**, employment by size class |
| Open Government data | [opendata.swiss](https://opendata.swiss/) | Datasets related to jobs/economy |
| Federal labour-market policy | SECO / [arbeit.swiss](https://www.arbeit.swiss) | Bund role, ALV, public job exchange |
| Cantonal public employment | RAV pages (start from arbeit.swiss) | Canton role; registered seekers |
| EU/CH comparison context | Eurostat labour market pages | Use only if relevant to DACH framing |
| Privacy law (product constraints) | Swiss nDSG materials; FDPIC (EDÖB) guidance | Product implications, not legal advice |
| GDPR orientation (if EU applicants/data) | Official GDPR text / EU commission pages | Flag cross-border issues |

## Trends & secondary research (Tier B — use carefully)

| Topic | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Skills-based hiring, apply spam, AI applications | Reputable HR/tech press; LinkedIn Economic Graph / workforce reports if public |
| Salary benchmarks CH | Official or well-known CH salary surveys (cite methodology) |
| Talent shortage by occupation | BFS + industry associations (ICT, banking, health) |
| Time to hire / cost per hire / turnover | Association HR reports, BFS labour, **sourced** consultancy studies (note methodology; often Tier B) |
| Channel revenue / share | JobCloud / listed-parent reports if public; never invent share |

| Global matching / talent startups | Product pages of non-EU players (analogues for Q21) — not CH market size |
