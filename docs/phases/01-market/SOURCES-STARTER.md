# Sources starter — Phase 01 (Market)

Use these as a **starting search list**, not an exhaustive bibliography. Verify every claim with URL + access date per [RESEARCH-STANDARD.md](../../../RESEARCH-STANDARD.md). Prefer Tier A/B.

## Official / statistical (Tier A)

| Topic | Where to look | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CH labour market stats | [BFS — Swiss Federal Statistical Office](https://www.bfs.admin.ch/) | Employment, unemployment, sectors |
| Open Government data | [opendata.swiss](https://opendata.swiss/) | Datasets related to jobs/economy |
| EU/CH comparison context | Eurostat labour market pages | Use only if relevant to DACH framing |
| Privacy law (product constraints) | Swiss nDSG materials; FDPIC (EDÖB) guidance | Product implications, not legal advice |
| GDPR orientation (if EU applicants/data) | Official GDPR text / EU commission pages | Flag cross-border issues |

## Job market channels to map (Tier A/B — primary observation)

Visit and document **role for seekers vs employers** (pricing pages if public):

| Channel | URL to start | Capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| jobs.ch | https://www.jobs.ch | Listing products, employer value prop, any public pricing |
| JobCloud / related brands | Corporate / product pages | Group structure, products |
| Indeed CH | https://ch.indeed.com | Apply volume dynamics, employer products |
| LinkedIn | https://www.linkedin.com | Easy Apply, Recruiter positioning (public pages) |
