## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Understand how Swiss hiring works — channels, journeys, institutions, pain, economics, roles, and what is changing.

Example questions — skip what stays thin after an honest search.

**Channels**

- Which channels from SOURCES-STARTER did you actually open? What else showed up (agencies, university portals, regional boards)?
- For major portals (jobs.ch, Indeed CH, LinkedIn, plus others you pick): what job does each do for **seekers** vs **employers**?
- What **public pricing** exists for employer products? If none, say so.
- Where do **career pages** and **public employment** (arbeit.swiss / RAV) fit?
- How does **multi-channel posting** create cost or noise for employers?

**Volume and shape**

- Roughly how many positions get filled in CH in a period you can name (BFS, SECO, opendata.swiss)?
- Where do **KMU** vs **large corporates** look for talent?
- **Time to hire** and **cost per hire** for a few industries — published vs `unknown`? (Listing price ≠ cost per hire.)
- Which occupations feel **tight** vs **slack**? Seasonal hiring? Sectors growing or shrinking?

**Journeys, institutions, pain**

- For a small company and a large one: how is a hiring need recognized; who **runs** the process; who **decides**?
- What role do **universities / FH / Berufsbildung**, the **Bund**, and **cantons / RAV** play?
- How do people find and choose employers? How much do **salary** and **culture** matter?
- What hurts on each side — and *where* in the journey?
- What did you learn from **applying yourself** (even one honest flow)?

**Roles, money, motion**

- Why do people leave? Turnover by industry if you can find it.
- Which roles are in high demand? Which feel **homogeneous** vs **heterogeneous**? Where is a **wrong hire** especially costly?
- Which channel looks **dominant**? How do channels **make money**?
- What **trends or changes** are visible? (If they matter for the product, pick them up in [01-T2](01-T2-PRODUCT-IMPLICATIONS.md).)
- Who is trying to **disrupt** matching or apply flows — in CH/DACH and elsewhere?

You might sketch tables if helpful: channels, market shape, journeys, institutions, roles, economics — not required forms.

**Start here**

1. [SOURCES-STARTER.md](../SOURCES-STARTER.md) — open channels as seeker and as employer.  
2. Sketch two journeys (KMU vs large; applicant).  
3. Try at least one real apply flow if you can ([APPLY-LOG.md](../evidence/APPLY-LOG.md) is optional).  
4. Then measure: BFS / SECO / association reports.  
5. Link your notes from the worksheet when you can talk through how hiring actually works.
