## Why this exists

Phase 01 can feel done when the story sounds good — even if numbers are unsourced or the brief’s statistics snuck in as facts.

T5 is the **closing pass**: make the phase usable for Gate 1 and Phase 02 by separating what we trust from what we must not treat as known.

This is **not** new research. It is hygiene and honesty before we leave the market phase.

---

## Our guideline

- **No new digging** — audit what T1–T4 already produced.
- **Sources on facts that matter.** Hard numbers, prices, legal claims need URL + access date or they get deleted / marked `unknown`.
- **Brief ≠ citation.** The brief’s 2–3k CHF listings and 40–50 applications are hypotheses until T1 sourced them.
- **Tier A first.** BFS, official pages, live pricing pages count; recap articles do not replace opening the source.
- **Worksheet stands alone.** Daniel should follow links from the worksheet to your notes and sources — not hunt through chat.

Log everything in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) as you work through T1–T4; T5 is when you make sure it is complete.

## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Close Phase 01 with a clean source log and a clear list of load-bearing unknowns.

Example questions:

**Sources**

- Does [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) list every URL you relied on in T1–T4 (with access dates)?
- Did you open **5–8 Tier A** sources before leaning on press and blogs? Which ones?
- Any starter links you never opened — remove them from the log.

**Numbers audit**

- Scan your notes for digits, “k CHF,” “%,” “months,” “applications.” Each one sourced, fixed, or gone?
- Any brief statistics still sitting in your text without a T1 source?

**Confidence check**

- What are **3–5 claims** you are most confident in?
- What are **3–5** you are least confident in?

**Load-bearing unknowns**

- Which unknowns must **later phases not treat as facts**? (Listing price, cost per hire, apply volume, market share, industry splits…)
- If Daniel dropped one unsupported claim tomorrow, which would change the story most?

**Handoff**

- What do you want to dig into **first in Phase 02** (competitors)?

**Start here**

1. Read [RESEARCH-STANDARD.md](../../../../RESEARCH-STANDARD.md) once if you have not.  
2. Digit-sweep T1–T4 + MARKET-BRIEF for orphan numbers.  
3. Complete [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).  
4. Write the short unknowns list.  
5. Tick T1–T5 on the worksheet when you can walk Daniel through the phase.
