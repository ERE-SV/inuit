## Why this exists

You can finish [T1](01-T1-MARKET-FUNCTIONS.md) with a solid map of Swiss hiring and still not know whether **this product idea** points at real friction or at a story we want to be true.

T2 is the **bridge**: turn market understanding into product intuition — where matchmaking might matter, where it would not, and what we should not claim yet.

---

## Our guideline

- **Build on T1 only.** No second research sprint — reflect on what you already saw.
- **Connect observation to implication.** “X is true” → “that might mean Y for us” → “I’m strong / weak / unsure about that.”
- **Test the brief, don’t defend it.** [docs/brief/01-vision.md](../../../brief/01-vision.md) is a hypothesis.
- **Stay exploratory.** A memo, bullets, or short table beats six identical cards.
- **Do not lock scope.** No MVP feature list, no beachhead, no pricing.

Good T2 sounds like: “Employers on jobs.ch buy reach, not screening — so our Top-X story needs a bootstrap path, not a listing replacement on day one.”  
Weak T2 sounds like: “AI is changing hiring” with nothing tied to T1.

## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Given what T1 showed, where might a two-sided matchmaking product matter — and where should we stay humble?

Example questions — follow what T1 made vivid; skip empty themes.

**Pain our idea might speak to**

- Where did you see **seeker** friction that a reusable profile and fewer blind applies might help?
- Where did you see **employer** friction that pre-qualified intros might help vs another listing slot?
- Which pains felt **strong** in T1 vs **weak or anecdotal**?

**Why matching might matter — or not**

- Does hiring look **keyword/CV driven**, or do structured requirements, commute, salary, culture already show up as real filters?
- Which roles felt **homogeneous** vs **heterogeneous**? Does that change how exciting matching is?
- Did anything suggest employers or seekers would **not** fill a rich profile — and why?

**Timing and liquidity**

- From T1’s volume / tightness / seasonality: does a wedge need to be local, seasonal, or niche?
- Anything that suggests early “empty marketplace” risk?

**Claims to hold lightly**

- Which brief ideas did T1 **support** (notify both sides, commute, less noise, structured match)?
- Which brief claims still look **unsupported** (listing price pain, 40–50 applications, “5–10× cheaper”)?
- What would you **not** put on a landing page yet?

**Optional lenses** (only if T1 gave you something)

- Labour motion · salary transparency · remote/commute · AI apply spam

You might sketch: **observation from T1** → **might mean for product** → **confidence** (strong / weak / unknown).

**Start here**

1. Re-read your T1 notes — whatever you actually wrote.  
2. Pick **3–5 observations** that stuck with you.  
3. For each: what might that mean for this product? What would you **not** claim yet?  
4. Note one place where the brief and T1 **disagree**.  
5. Link your note from the worksheet.
