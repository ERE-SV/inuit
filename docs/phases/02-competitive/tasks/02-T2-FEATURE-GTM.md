## Why this exists

White space is empty if you only know names. You need a side-by-side on **product** (how matching/listing actually works) and **go-to-market** (how they acquire employers and seekers). That contrast feeds Phase 02 white space and Phase 04 GTM (“we are not another self-serve listing”). Comparing all ≥8 in depth is fine; a documented top subset plus “others summarized” is also fine.

## Our guideline

- **Build on T1.** Reuse the competitor set (or a documented subset) — do not swap names without updating T1.
- **Observe both sides.** Open each player as seeker and as employer where you can; public product pages beat marketing adjectives.
- **Keep product and GTM together.** Matching vs listing is both a **product** difference and a **GTM** difference.
- **Mark the unobservable.** Structured match? Employer motion? Seeker motion? Use `unknown` after a real look — not a blank cell.
- **Do not position yet.** Capture what *they* do; save “what we might own” for [T4](02-T4-WHITE-SPACE.md).

Closest analogue ≠ “nobody does matching.” Aggregators, agencies, and talent marketplaces may already forward candidates.

## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Compare rivals side-by-side on product mechanism and go-to-market — so gaps are gaps in *behavior*, not slogans.

Example questions — follow what T1 made vivid; skip empty columns.

**Product**

- For each compared player: primary user (seeker / employer / both), core mechanism (listing, Easy Apply, agency search, ATS workflow, matching), and public value proposition in their own words (quote or paraphrase + URL)?
- Who supports structured requirements / preference matching (commute, salary floor, culture tags) vs keyword search and free-text ads? Note `unknown` if not observable.

**Go-to-market**

- How do employers typically buy or start (self-serve listing, sales, recruiter seats, agency retainer)? Evidence or `unknown`.
- How do seekers typically arrive (SEO, brand, network effects, university, etc.)? Evidence or labeled **inference**.

**Analogues to our idea**

- Where does the brief’s “forward candidates before full employer onboarding” pattern already exist (if anywhere)? What is the closest analogue?

You might sketch columns: player · user · mechanism · VP (quoted) · structured match? · employer motion · seeker motion.
