## Why this exists

Phase 03’s recommendation needs a gap incumbents actually leave open — tied to matrix observations, not “we will do AI matching.” Positioning here is a **draft input**, not a locked brand (that is Gate 3). Mixing analysis (facts about others) with our slogan in one blurry paragraph is the usual failure.

---

## Our guideline

- **First analyze, then draft.** Re-read T1–T3 observations before writing how *we* might differ.
- **Tie gaps to evidence.** Each white-space bullet should point at a specific competitor behavior from the matrix.
- **Separate analysis from positioning.** Keep “what they do” and “what we might own” visibly distinct.
- **Name what is not white space.** Strong incumbents (volume SEO, Easy Apply, agency relationships) are not gaps — do not pretend to win there soon.
- **Messaging caution.** Name the noise problem without unsubstantiated HR-bashing. See [docs/brief/03-gtm-niche.md](../../../brief/03-gtm-niche.md).

“AI matching” with no observation that rivals fail on noise, commute, or structured requirements is wishful.
