## Why this exists

Later Phase 02 work and Phase 03’s “why not agencies/portals alone” need a real set of rivals, not a vibe that LinkedIn is big. A usable set has enough rows to compare (≥8 is a useful bar), mixes **direct** (same job: fill/find roles) and **indirect** (agency, ATS, aggregator, employer brand), and says why each name is in. Thin sets make white space and beachhead scoring guesswork.

---

## Our guideline

- **Start from Phase 01.** Channels, disruptors, and segment signals from the market map should drive who you include.
- **Open live sites.** [COMPETITORS-STARTER.md](../COMPETITORS-STARTER.md) is a seed list, not the answer — swap stale names; keep a starting URL per row.
- **Type each name.** **Direct** = same core job (listings or matching). **Indirect** = different product, same budget or habit (agency, ATS, aggregator, employer brand).
- **Be honest about gaps.** If no CH matching analogue qualifies yet, say what you opened — do not invent one.
- **Do not position yet.** No white space, no tagline, no “we beat X.”

Do not treat Kununu/Glassdoor as a job board without saying they are brand/reviews. “≥8 / ≥3 direct / ≥3 indirect” is a quality bar, not a form to fill on this page.
