## Why this exists

Later monetization and messaging will try to say we are cheaper or better value. That claim dies if Phase 02 invented CHF. Every pricing cell should be a **sourced figure** or **`unknown`**. This also stops a false “5–10× cheaper than portals” story from hardening before anyone opened a price page.

---

## Our guideline

- **Open employer pricing pages.** For each T1 player, try the employer/product/pricing path — then write number + URL + access date, or `unknown` plus what you opened.
- **Treat the brief as a hypothesis.** The brief’s ~2–3k CHF per position is something to **validate**, not copy. Never paste the brief as a source.
- **`unknown` is finished work.** A blank cell is not. `unknown` after you opened the employer pricing/product page counts.
- **Separate listing price from cost per hire.** Listing *price* is not cost *per hire* — do not mix them in the same cell.
- **Label inference.** “Too expensive” belongs in a note, labeled **inference** — not in the price cell.

Do not invent pricing commitments that Phase 04 or later phases will treat as facts.
