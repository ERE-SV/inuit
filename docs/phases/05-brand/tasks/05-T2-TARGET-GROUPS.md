## Why this exists

Brand must work on **both** sides of the marketplace. Tone and visuals need named beachhead segments — not “everyone in Switzerland.”

---

## Our guideline

- **Both sides, one company.** Ignoring the company side yields a seeker-only personality that fails employer UI and sales conversations.
- **Tie names to Gate 1.** Use the locked beachhead niche × geography — do not invent a new segment here.
- **Label inference.** One fear per side is enough if you say when it is your read vs sourced.
- **One shared trust idea.** Brand tone and visuals should reinforce the same signal on both sides.
- **Name who is out of scope.** Explicit “not for brand focus now” keeps Gate 3 lean.
