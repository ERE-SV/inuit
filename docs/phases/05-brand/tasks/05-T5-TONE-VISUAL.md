## Why this exists

Gate 3 needs tone rules and visual principles specific enough that a designer can start Figma — and naming direction (options OK). Lean pack, not a full brand book.

---

## Our guideline

- **Lean and binding.** About 1–2 pages of usable rules — cut anything that blocks Gate 3 on logo explorations.
- **Tone with teeth.** Cover claims about AI/matching, talking about HR, salary, and fairness — match GTM do/don’t lines.
- **Visuals must be specific.** Color intent, typography intent, layout/density, imagery, and an **explicit avoid** list — not “modern and clean.”
- **Naming can stay open.** Options or criteria are enough; principles still lock even if the final name does not.
- **Show, don’t tell.** One example UI sentence for seeker and one for company, written in the proposed tone.

Pitfalls: vague visuals; tone that bashes HR without evidence; accidentally starting a full design system.
