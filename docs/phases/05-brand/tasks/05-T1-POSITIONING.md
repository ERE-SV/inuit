- **Obey GTM boundaries.** Messaging caution from the brief and locked GTM (e.g. no unsubstantiated HR-bashing) applies here.
- **Propose, don’t lock.** Daniel approves Gate 3; cite decision IDs where you rely on locked choices.

## What we're doing now

**Goal:** Draft a promise / positioning line and “we are not” statements grounded in the locked beachhead and competitive landscape.

Example questions — skip what stays thin after an honest read of prior work.

- In your own words: what is the difference between **positioning** and **branding** for this product?
- After competitive work: which **3 alternatives** does a beachhead buyer compare you to (portals, LinkedIn, agencies, ATS+boards, etc.)?
- Draft a **promise / positioning line** (who + problem + how we are different). Keep it short (e.g. ≤25 words).
- List a few **“we are not”** statements (e.g. not another listing slot; not a full ATS). Why each matters for trust.
- What messaging caution from GTM/brief must the brand obey (e.g. no unsubstantiated HR-bashing)?

You might sketch: one promise line + 3 “we are not” bullets + alternatives table — optional, not a form.

**Start here**

1. Open Gate 1–2 decisions and Phase 02 positioning / matrix.

3. Draft promise + “we are not”; cite paths / decision IDs where you rely on locked choices.
