## Why this exists

Personality should follow the jobs people want done — not generic “innovation.” A short we-are / we-are-not table becomes the backbone of tone and visual mood.

---

## Our guideline

- **JTBD = progress wanted.** Use verb phrases (“hire faster without drowning in CVs”), not feature lists.
- **Link traits to jobs.** Every personality trait should explain which job it makes believable.
- **Avoid fluff.** Skip words with no JTBD link (e.g. “innovative” without saying what job that serves).
- **Differentiate from competitors.** Note one competitor personality from Phase 02 to avoid copying without reason.
- **Small set.** Pick roughly 3–5 traits — enough for T5 to operationalize, not a thesaurus.
