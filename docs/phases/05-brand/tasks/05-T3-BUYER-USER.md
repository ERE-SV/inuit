## Why this exists

“HR” is not one person. Tone for an economic buyer, a daily user, and a blocker can differ. Map roles so brand and GTM do not talk past the wrong person.

## Our guideline

- **Do not re-research personas.** Start from Gate 2 [04-T4 buyer map](../../04-gtm/tasks/04-T4-BUYER-SALES.md) — translate into voice/tone implications only.
- **Separate buyer, user, blocker.** Who pays / decides vs who clicks daily vs who can stop a deal.
- **Use GTM evidence.** Cite Phase 04 buyer notes or public role descriptions; label inference.
- **Decide voice early.** One voice vs slight variants for seeker UI vs employer UI — justify briefly so Figma does not invent three personalities.
- **Note what scares blockers.** Legal, IT, works council, board — brand promise must not trigger unnecessary fear.
- **Beachhead-specific.** A KMU CEO-buyer is not a bank HR shared-services buyer; map the locked segment.
