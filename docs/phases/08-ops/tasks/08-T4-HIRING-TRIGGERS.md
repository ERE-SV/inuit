## Our guideline

- **Measurable triggers only.** Volume, breakage rate, hours/week, GTM milestones — not vibes.
- **Tie to GTM.** Sales hire #1/#2 should connect to forwards/week or onboarded employers from Gate 2.
- **Founder + one hire boundary.** What does that pair run vs what waits for revenue or funding?
- **Show Daniel a table.** Trigger → Hire is the deliverable shape.
- **Do not invent thresholds.** Tie numbers to ops model and GTM where you can; label assumptions.
