# Minutes

Post-session minutes go here (`YYYY-MM-DD-*.md`). Use [templates/minutes.md](../templates/minutes.md).

Keep minutes short: **decisions, actions, blockers, next focus**. Put lasting research in `docs/phases/`. Full recordings/text go in [transcripts/](../transcripts/).

T-24 packs (`YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-t24.md`) and gate briefs (`YYYY-MM-DD-gate-N-brief.md`) also live here — they are pre-reads, not minutes.
